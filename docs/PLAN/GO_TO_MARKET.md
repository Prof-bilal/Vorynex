## 1. GTM Framework

### 1.1 GTM Principles

1. **Product-Led:** The product itself is the primary driver of growth
2. **Developer-First:** Focus on developers, not procurement or security teams
3. **Education-First:** Educate the market on post-exploitation containment
4. **Community-Driven:** Build and leverage developer communities
5. **Data-Driven:** Measure everything, iterate quickly
6. **Customer-Centric:** Obsess over customer success

### 1.2 GTM Pillars

| Pillar | Description | Owner | Metrics |
|--------|-------------|-------|---------|
| **Product** | Build a product users love | Engineering | NPS, Retention, Usage |
| **Growth** | Acquire and activate users | Marketing | Signups, Activation, Conversion |
| **Revenue** | Monetize and retain customers | Sales | Revenue, ARPU, Expansion |
| **Experience** | Delight customers | Customer Success | CSAT, Support, Onboarding |
| **Ecosystem** | Build partnerships and integrations | Partnerships | Partners, Integrations, Revenue |

### 1.3 GTM Funnel

```
    AWARENESS          CONSIDERATION          DECISION          RETENTION          EXPANSION
    ─────────           ─────────────           ───────           ────────           ────────
       │                      │                     │                 │                │
       ▼                      ▼                     ▼                 ▼                ▼
┌─────────────┐       ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐   ┌─────────────┐  ┌─────────────┐
│  100,000    │──────▶│   10,000    │────▶│    1,000    │──▶│    800      │─▶│    500      │
│  Visitors   │       │   Leads     │     │   Trials    │   │   Customers  │  │  Expanded    │
└─────────────┘       └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘   └─────────────┘  └─────────────┘
       │                      │                     │                 │                │
       ▼                      ▼                     ▼                 ▼                ▼
   $0.50 CPC        $50 CPA            5% Conversion    80% Retention   120% NRR
```

**Funnel Metrics:**
- **Awareness:** Visitors, impressions, reach
- **Consideration:** Leads, MQLs (Marketing Qualified Leads)
- **Decision:** Trials, SQLs (Sales Qualified Leads), customers
- **Retention:** Active customers, churn rate
- **Expansion:** Upsells, cross-sells, referrals

### 1.4 GTM Model

**Primary Model:** Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- **80% of revenue** from self-service and low-touch
- **20% of revenue** from sales-assisted

**Secondary Model:** Sales-Assisted
- **Enterprise customers** (50+ workloads)
- **Custom deployments** (on-prem, air-gapped)
- **High-touch onboarding** (dedicated CSM)

**Hybrid Approach:**
- **PLG for SMBs:** Self-service, freemium, usage-based
- **Sales for Enterprise:** High-touch, custom pricing, dedicated support

## 2. Phase 1: Launch and Validation (Months 0-6)

**Goal:** Launch Vorynex, validate product-market fit, and achieve early traction

**Theme:** "Build in Public, Launch with Community"

### 2.1 Objectives

### 2.2 Key Activities

#### 2.2.1 Product Launch
- **Alpha Launch (Month 0-1):**
  - Launch to personal network (50-100 users)
  - Collect feedback, iterate quickly
  - Focus on stability and usability
  
- **Beta Launch (Month 2-3):**
  - Public beta announcement
  - Waitlist for early access
  - Feature freeze, bug fixes only
  - Collect NPS, usage data
  
- **General Availability (Month 4-5):**
  - Official launch announcement
  - Press release, blog post, social media
  - Launch event (virtual or in-person)

- **ICP Interviews:** 20-30 interviews with target customers
- **Problem Validation:** Confirm pain points and needs

- **Recruitment:** Target 100-200 early adopters
- **Onboarding:** Personalized onboarding for each user
- **Feedback:** Collect feedback via surveys, interviews, usage data
- **Incentives:** Early adopter badge, free credits, swag
- **Success Stories:** Collect testimonials, case studies

#### 2.2.4 Community Building
- **Launch Community:** Slack, Discord, or GitHub Discussions
- **Content:** Blog posts, tutorials, videos, documentation
- **Engagement:** AMAs, office hours, community calls
- **Evangelism:** Encourage community members to share Vorynex

#### 2.2.5 Content and Education

- **Documentation:** Complete docs, guides, tutorials
- **Videos:** Demo videos, tutorials, webinars

## 7. Pricing and Packaging Strategy

### 7.1 Pricing Model

**Model:** Hybrid (Freemium + Usage-based + Subscription)

**Tiers:**
1. **Free:** 0-2 workloads, basic features
2. **Pay-As-You-Go:** $25/workload/month (3-20 workloads)
3. **Subscription:** $100/month + $20/additional workload
4. **Enterprise:** Custom pricing (50+ workloads)

### 7.2 Packaging Strategy

**Free Tier:**
- Observe mode
- Basic protection
- 1-2 workloads
- Community support
- 7-day event retention

**Pay-As-You-Go:**
- All Free features
- Protect mode
- Advanced protection
- Emergency Shield
- Blast Radius
- CVE correlation
- Email support
- 30-day event retention
- Multi-language (Beta)

**Subscription:**
- All Pay-As-You-Go features
- 5 workloads included
- Priority email support
- 90-day event retention
- Multi-language support
- Slack support
- Team management
- Audit logs

**Enterprise:**
- All Subscription features
- Custom workload limit
- SSO (SAML, OIDC)
- RBAC
- Compliance reports
- Dedicated support
- Dedicated CSM
- Custom policies
- API access
- Multi-cloud
- Kubernetes

### 7.3 Pricing Validation

**Validation Methodology:**
- Competitive benchmarking
- Customer interviews
- Survey data
- A/B testing
- Willingness-to-pay analysis

**Van Westendorp Results:**
- Too Cheap: $10
- Good Deal: $25
- Expensive: $50
- Too Expensive: $100

**Optimal Price Range:** $25 - $50 per workload

## 8. Sales Strategy

### 8.1 Sales Team Structure

**Year 1:**
- 1 Sales Lead (Founder or hire)
- 1 Account Executive
- 1 Solution Engineer

**Year 2:**
- 1 Sales Manager
- 2 Account Executives
- 1 Solution Engineer
- 1 SDR (Sales Development Rep)

**Year 3:**
- 1 Sales Director
- 3 Account Executives
- 2 Solution Engineers
- 2 SDRs
- 1 Sales Ops

### 8.2 Sales Process

**PLG Sales Process:**
1. **Monitor:** Track PLG usage and signals
2. **Identify:** Identify high-value PLG users
3. **Engage:** Reach out with personalized message
4. **Qualify:** Assess fit and readiness
5. **Demo:** Show value of enterprise features
6. **Propose:** Custom pricing and terms
7. **Close:** Negotiate and close deal
8. **Onboard:** Ensure successful adoption

**Enterprise Sales Process:**
1. **Prospect:** Identify target accounts
2. **Research:** Understand account needs
3. **Outreach:** Initial contact (email, call, LinkedIn)
4. **Qualify:** Assess fit and readiness (BANT, MEDDIC)
5. **Discovery:** Deep-dive into needs and pain points
6. **Demo:** Custom demo tailored to account
7. **Proposal:** Custom proposal with pricing
8. **Negotiation:** Negotiate terms and pricing
9. **Close:** Sign contract, collect payment
10. **Onboard:** Ensure successful adoption
11. **Renew:** Manage relationship, drive expansion
